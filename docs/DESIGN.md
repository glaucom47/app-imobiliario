## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the tension between architectural restraint and high-stakes financial velocity. Tailored for top-tier real estate advisors and agency principals transacting prime Portuguese real estate (from Quinta do Lago estates to Príncipe Real palacetes), the interface evokes the quiet authority of an architectural monograph crossed with the cryptographic precision of modern private wealth tools.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with **Tactile Precision and Editorial Layout**:
- **Atmosphere**: Calm, sovereign, and razor-sharp. It removes all broker clichés (generic banners, neon badges, cluttered widgets) in favor of museum-grade breathing room, crisp typographical hierarchies, and physical paper-like tactility.
- **Emotional Intent**: Confidence under pressure during critical transaction phases (CPCV, Escritura, due diligence) and effortless luxury during client presentations. The visual language conveys that the consultant operating the tool handles millions of euros with calm, disciplined mastery.
- **Sunlight Readability**: Extreme luminance contrast ensures instant legibility on bright terraces in Cascais, Algarve golf courses, or direct sunlit construction visits.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in pristine monochrome with structural mineral tones and intentional metallic/chromatic accents:

- **Primary (`#111111`)**: Deep Onyx. Used for primary typography, authoritative actions, structural solid buttons, and key framing elements. Never pure synthetic `#000000`; Deep Onyx preserves optical warmth.
- **Secondary (`#C5A880`)**: Champagne Ore. A subdued, warm brushed gold accent evoking architectural brass, limestone fixtures, and premier editorial print. Reserved for prestige indicators, pipeline deal closures, VIP tags, and subtle progress fills. Never used for loud body text or raw decorative clutter.
- **Tertiary (`#1D4ED8`)**: Deep Cobalt. Borrowed from high-performance financial systems for definitive, high-intent interactive states: executing digital signings, initiating CPCV validation, audio recording triggers, and active system confirmations.
- **Neutral (`#FBFBFB`)**: Warm Alabaster canvas. Paired with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces to build gentle depth without heavy drop shadows.
- **Hairline Framing (`#EAEAEA` and `#E2E4E8`)**: Titanium hairline dividers (0.5px to 1px) that provide architectural perimeter definition without visual heaviness.
- **Semantic Signals**: Muted Olive Emerald (`#1B4332`) for notarized steps/CPCV signatures; Deep Crimson (`#991B1B`) for legal discrepancies or missing IMT receipts.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans was selected for its geometric purity, generous counter-spaces, and contemporary, architectural feel that rivals custom European bespoke typefaces.

- **Tabular Figures (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'cv01' on`)**: Mandatory across every surface rendering currency values (€), tax simulations (IMT, Imposto do Selo), areas (m²), commission splits, and countdown timers to Escritura. Numbers align cleanly in vertical matrices.
- **Editorial Contrast**: Headlines utilize deliberate tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and substantial weight, while section tags, procedural stages, and status indicators use micro uppercase tracking (`+0.08em`) to mimic luxury print indexes.
- **Hierarchy Integrity**: Deal valuations (e.g., `€ 2.850.000`) always lead with assertive scale (`financial-display`), keeping currency symbols slightly muted in tone to let the quantity command focus.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a fluid grid bounded by structured safe areas:
- **Mobile First Framework**: 4-column fluid system on mobile (viewport `< 768px`) with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding to protect against bezel occlusion. 12-column adaptive layout on desktop/tablets.
- **Rhythm**: Anchored by an 8pt base unit. Small gaps (4px/8px) link related financial parameters; broader clearances (24px/40px) segment transaction stages (e.g., from Angariação contract review down to Notarial scheduling).
- **Edge Containment**: Cards touch full viewport width with razor hairline margins or float with uniform horizontal margins on mobile devices to preserve natural thumb zones.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the design system is architectural rather than skeuomorphic:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Base`: `#FBFBFB` (Warm Alabaster).
  - `Tier 1 (Cards, Modules)`: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting with a hairline border (`1px solid #EAEAEA`).
  - `Tier 2 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Action Banners)`: `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.05), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.03)`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: Borders never exceed 1px, and where hardware permits, 0.5px rendering is preferred. Elevation relies on subtle tonal contrast (white on alabaster) punctuated by crisp borders rather than dark Drop Shadows.
- **Tactile Sheets**: Bottom sheets for recording audio logs and scripts glide into the frame with a satin backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%); background: rgba(251, 251, 251, 0.82)`), evoking Apple Wallet cards.

## Shapes
Roundedness sits deliberately at level `2` (Rounded):
- Standard cards, sheet containers, and primary buttons employ `0.5rem` (8px) to `1rem` (16px) corner radiuses.
- This creates crisp, structural discipline that respects modern hardware contours without sliding into playful pill-shapes or cold brutalist right angles.
- Specialized pills are used exclusively for compact transaction status tags (e.g., `EM DUE DILIGENCE`, `ESCRITURA MARCADA`).

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary Transaction Button**: Deep Onyx background (`#111111`), pristine white typography, `rounded-lg`, 52px height for decisive ergonomics. Hover/Press state transitions to charcoal tint with zero bouncy scale artifacts.
- **Cobalt Action Trigger**: Deep Cobalt (`#1D4ED8`) for primary real-time actions: *Validar CPCV*, *Gerar Simulação IMT*, *Gravar Relatório de Visita*.
- **Secondary Ghost**: Clear background with a subtle titanium hairline outline (`#E2E4E8`), Onyx text.
- **Audio Log Record Button**: Haptic round trigger with micro pulse rings, dedicated to verbal client debriefs.

### 2. Transaction Pipeline Cards (Angariações & Fecho)
- Elevated `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by `#EAEAEA`.
- Divided into two clear architectural zones:
  - *Header*: Typographic hierarchy showcasing property location (e.g., "Avenida da Liberdade, T3 Duplex") with a Champagne Ore accent pill indicating commission potential.
  - *Metrics Ribbon*: Tabular numbers displaying asking price, simulated IMT + Imposto do Selo total, and closing date countdown.

### 3. Financial & Legal Breakdown Matrix (IMT & Escritura)
- Clean, lined data list with zero vertical separators.
- Alternating subtle rows where numeric values are aligned right in tabular format (`tnum`).
- Instant toggles between *Habitação Própria Permanente* (HPP) and *Habitação Secundária/Arrendamento* with segmented titanium pill switchers.

### 4. Chips & Procedural Status
- Compact caps styling (`label-caps`).
- Dynamic border-tone pairing:
  - *CPCV Pendente*: `#C5A880` background at 10% opacity, text in deepened bronze.
  - *Escritura Concluída*: `#1B4332` background at 8% opacity, forest green text.
  - *Visita Realizada*: Neutral titanium background with charcoal typography.

### 5. Input Fields & Selectors
- Pristine white fill, flat 1px slate-gray perimeter (`#EAEAEA`), transitioning on focus to a precise Deep Onyx border (`#111111`) with no neon halos.
- Floating microscopic uppercase labels (`label-caps`) that retain context while entering multi-digit euro sums.

### 6. Video Script & Teleprompter Drawer
- Dark-mode contextual surface option (`#111111` solid backdrop) designed to maintain high readability while filming WhatsApp/Instagram video walk-throughs for high-net-worth buyers, featuring adjustable body-lg font sizing and scroll pacing.
